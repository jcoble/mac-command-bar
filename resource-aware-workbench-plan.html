Assumptions and locked decisions

complete
  • The dirty worktree is preserved and reviewed; unrelated work is never reverted or swept into a commit.
  • SQLite owns Assembly-created durable state; external systems keep their own authority.
  • Managed image/proof binaries stay on disk with authoritative SQLite metadata.
  • Only open tabs in the active Assembly session intentionally retain multiple EditorStates.
  • Monaco leaves after CodeMirror parity; the Notion panel is native and optional.
  • Tree-sitter is not added; CodeMirror parsers and LSPs provide language behavior.
  • Codex chat is the interaction benchmark; Spotify supplies hierarchy, not branding or universal spacing.
  • Visible UI styling tries a verified Opus 5 lane first, then Codex/native agents, always against populated fixtures and final real-app proof.
